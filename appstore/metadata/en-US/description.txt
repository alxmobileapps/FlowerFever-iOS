Flower Fever is a relaxing match-3 puzzle game full of roses, sunflowers and daisies — and a garden that grows with you.

Swap two flowers to match 3 or more of the same kind. Finish each level's flower order before you run out of moves, earn up to 3 stars, and watch your garden bloom.

MATCH & BLOOM
• Hundreds of hand-made levels with gentle goals
• Big, clear flowers that are easy to see
• Power-ups: Striped blooms, Bouquets, Busy bees and Rainbow flowers
• Combine power-ups for giant blasts
• A joyful Flower Shower when you finish a level

GROW YOUR GARDEN
• Win levels to earn flower coins and water
• Plant seeds, watch them grow, and pick your flowers
• Unlock ponds, a pergola and flowering trees
• Collect more than 130 kinds of flowers, from common daisies to legendary blooms

MAKE BOUQUETS
• Use the flowers you pick to make 36 beautiful bouquets
• Fill 3 bouquet albums and earn gifts
• Save or share your bouquet pictures

PLAY WITH FAMILY & FRIENDS
• Share your Player ID and visit each other's gardens
• Water a friend's garden and send small coin gifts every day

HELPFUL & FRIENDLY
• Lifelines when you get stuck
• Daily gifts and weekly challenges
• Optional Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress on all your devices

Flower Fever is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (diamond packs and No Ads).
