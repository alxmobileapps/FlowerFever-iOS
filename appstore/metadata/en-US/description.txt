Relax, match and bloom! Flower Fever is a cozy match-3 puzzle game with big, bright flowers and a garden that grows with you.

Swap two flowers to match 3 or more. Finish each level's goals before you run out of moves, earn up to 3 stars, and enjoy a joyful Flower Shower at the end of every win.

MATCH & BLOOM
• 1,000 levels across 20 beautiful gardens
• Big, clear flowers that are easy to see
• Clear soil, ice, ponds, garden stones and beehives
• Power-ups: Striped blooms, Bouquets, Busy Bees and Rainbow flowers
• Combine power-ups for giant chain reactions — can you get an "Unbelievable!"?

GROW YOUR OWN GARDEN
• Win levels to earn flower coins and water
• Plant seeds, watch them grow and pick your flowers
• Collect more than 130 kinds of flowers, from common daisies to legendary blooms
• Unlock ponds, a pergola and flowering trees

MAKE BOUQUETS
• Turn the flowers you pick into 36 beautiful bouquets
• Fill 3 bouquet albums and earn gifts
• Save or share your bouquet pictures

PLAY WITH FAMILY & FRIENDS
• Visit each other's gardens with your Player ID
• Water a friend's garden and send daily gifts
• Ask friends for the flower you need — and send flowers back

MADE FOR RELAXING PLAY
• Simple, big buttons and few words
• Friendly voices cheer you on — choose from 4 voices
• Lifelines when you get stuck
• Daily gifts and weekly challenges
• Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress on all your devices

Flower Fever is free to play. It contains ads and optional in-app purchases (diamond packs and No Ads).
